# Waypoint BTW DTW nm Heading Length nm TWA Current
Bearings are true. BTW/DTW: bearing and distance from the boat to each point. Leg heading/length: from the previous point of the course. TWA is for the leg in the current true wind direction, assuming it holds: green to starboard, red to port. On the point being sailed to, the TWA on the bearing from here is shown beneath. Current, when known, is an arrow relative to the leg: up with it, down against it, sideways across it, and thicker the stronger it is. The next point is highlighted. BTW/DTW published by the server's Course API or a plotter over NMEA are shown below the table. A waypoint moved since the route was made is flagged: the route keeps its own copy of the position, which is what is shown and navigated to.
