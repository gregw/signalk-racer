Speeds in knots. Polar ratio: a sample's VMG over the polar VMG in that direction, for the wind at the time. Speed ratio: the boat's speed over the polar speed at the angle sailed, pooled across all directions. Tuned performance factor: the performance factor, blended towards the pooled speed ratio and then the direction's own polar ratio as samples arrive. Best VMG = polar VMG × tuned factor (or the sample percentile without a polar). The large number is the effective VMG: the best of the best VMG, the VMG now, and the floor. The row forcing it is highlighted in bold yellow, and the rows the best VMG comes from in yellow. Directions outlined in yellow are the legs the time to line is built from.
